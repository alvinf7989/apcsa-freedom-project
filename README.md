# APCSA Freedom Project
by **Alvin Frias**

## Context
I am a student at [HSTAT](https://www.hstat.org/) in the [Software Engineering Program](https://hstatsep.github.io/). The "Freedom Project" for AP Computer Science A is a year-long project all about making anything using technology.

For my project, I chose to independently study **Godot** in order to help me make a **Vice Versa 2D Shooter Game**. I thought of adding a unique twist to usual 2d Shooters like Galaga and has more ships on the bottom rather than on the top and one ship up top that's shooting. Now, you may think that this won't be fair for the top ship, but I increased the health for that top ship to be more than the player's ship to add difficult.

## Implications
##### PROS
* **Social**
    * People will like the unique twist that I put compared to normal 2d shooters.
    * Some people like the visuals added during the gameplay.
* **Game**
    * This will make 2d shooters in general more aware.
    * Shows the unique twist where there's a vice versa going on in the 2D Shooter project.
* **Global**
    * Much like almost every project I've made, no physical assets have been destroyed in the making of this project.
##### CONS
* **Social**
    * People will find it a bit difficult without knowing the rapid spacebar strategy.
    * People will become bored of playing 2d Shooters in general.
* **Game**
    * There's only one level in the game.
    * There are no instructions anywhere in the game.
* **Global**
    * This is only accessible through my laptop with Godot.
